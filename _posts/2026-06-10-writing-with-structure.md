Strong structure makes a blog feel calm and trustworthy.

For most technical or reflective posts, this pattern works well:

1. What problem are you solving?
2. What did you try?
3. What worked, what did not?
4. What should the reader do next?

If a post gets long, break it into small sections with clear subheads.

## What readers want

Readers usually want one of three things:

- A clear answer
- A useful example
- A reason to keep exploring

This template gives you room for all three without adding clutter.
